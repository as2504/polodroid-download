# Polodroid - Android download

A privacy-first camera for Android: your photos stay on your phone unless you choose otherwise.

**Download:** [polodroid.apk](https://github.com/as2504/polodroid-download/raw/main/polodroid.apk) (always the latest version)

## Install
1. Open the downloaded `polodroid.apk` on your Android phone (Android 8 or newer).
2. Allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager when asked.
3. If Play Protect warns that the app is unrecognised, choose **Install anyway** - Polodroid isn't in the Play Store yet.

To update, download and install again: your photos and settings stay.
